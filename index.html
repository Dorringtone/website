Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Browse the products, add what you need to your cart, then enter your delivery details. Open WhatsApp from the cart, review the prepared message and press Send. We’ll confirm your order on WhatsApp.

Do I need an account to order?

No. You can browse, use the cart and send an order request on WhatsApp without registering or logging in.

Do you deliver to my neighbourhood?

We serve addresses within 10 km of our Adams Arcade stall in Nairobi, subject to address and road access confirmation. Enter your estate or neighbourhood in the cart and add a landmark if it helps us find you.

Does the cart subtotal include delivery?

No. We confirm product availability, the delivery fee, timing, the final total and payment arrangements on WhatsApp.
