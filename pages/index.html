Shopping questions

How do I place an order?

Choose your produce, add your delivery details in the cart and open WhatsApp. Review the prepared message and press Send so we can confirm your order.

Does the subtotal include delivery?

The subtotal covers your produce. We confirm availability, delivery fees, timing and the final total over WhatsApp.

Do you deliver to my neighbourhood?

We serve addresses within 10 km of Adams Arcade, subject to address and road access. See delivery details.
